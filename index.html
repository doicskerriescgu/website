Who We Are

Volunteers at the Heart of the Community

Skerries Coast Guard is a unit of the Irish Coast Guard (IRCG), operating under the Department of Transport. Our team of highly trained volunteers is a dedicated search and drone unit, responding to emergencies of every kind — along cliffs, coastlines, and at inland rivers and lakes — across North County Dublin.

We work alongside An Garda Síochána, the National Ambulance Service, RNLI and the Dublin Fire Brigade to ensure that those in danger on the coast receive a fast, professional response.
