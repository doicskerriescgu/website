Services

What We Do

From drone operations to ground searches, our trained volunteers respond to a wide range of coastal emergencies.

Drone Operations

Skerries Coast Guard operates UAV drones as a primary search and reconnaissance tool. Our drone team can rapidly survey large stretches of coastline, locate missing persons in terrain inaccessible on foot, and relay live video to incident commanders — dramatically cutting search times and improving safety for our ground teams.

  • Aerial search over coastline, beaches, and open ground
  • Thermal imaging for low-light and night searches
  • Live video feed to incident command
  • Rapid deployment — airborne within minutes of activation
  • Coordination with IRCG helicopter service (Rescue 116)
🔎

Search & Rescue

Search and rescue is about far more than locating missing persons. Skerries Coast Guard responds to emergencies of every kind across our patch — along clifftops and cliff faces, on beaches and coastlines, and at inland rivers and lakes. Whether it's a casualty stranded on a cliff, a person in difficulty in the water, or someone reported missing, we activate a coordinated operation combining drone aerial coverage with systematic ground searches, working alongside An Garda Síochána and IRCG helicopters.

  • Response to emergencies along cliffs, coastlines, inland rivers and lakes
  • Cliff and shoreline casualty location and support
  • Systematic ground and waterside searches
  • Drone aerial coverage in parallel with ground teams
  • Multi-agency coordination
  • Night search capability with drone thermal imaging
🚁

Helicopter Operations

Skerries Coast Guard works hand in hand with the Irish Coast Guard's search and rescue helicopters. Our volunteers are trained to set up and secure helicopter landing zones, manage ground safety around an aircraft, and support winch operations during a rescue. This close working relationship lets us move casualties quickly to definitive care.

  • Establishing and securing helicopter landing zones (HLZ)
  • Ground safety procedures around live aircraft
  • Flight and winch familiarisation training
  • Direct support to IRCG helicopter crews during rescues
🛌

First Aid & Casualty Care

All Skerries Coast Guard volunteers are trained in emergency first aid and CPR. Our teams carry first aid equipment and are able to provide immediate pre-hospital care to casualties at the scene while awaiting or coordinating with the National Ambulance Service.

  • Emergency first aid and CPR
  • Casualty assessment and stabilisation
  • Hypothermia management
  • Liaison with National Ambulance Service
📡

Multi-Agency Support

Skerries Coast Guard provides drone reconnaissance and search support to other emergency services operating on the North Dublin coast. Our aerial capability is a valuable asset to An Garda Síochána, the National Ambulance Service, Dublin Fire Brigade, and the IRCG helicopter service during major incidents.

  • Drone support to Garda search operations
  • Aerial reconnaissance for NAS and DFB incidents
  • Communications relay and scene monitoring
  • Ground team tasking based on drone intelligence
🚩

Community Safety & Education

Prevention is at the core of our mission. Skerries Coast Guard volunteers regularly visit local schools, sports clubs, and community groups to deliver water safety education. We also participate in public events, beach patrols during busy summer periods, and safety demonstrations.

  • School visits and water safety talks
  • Beach safety patrols in summer
  • Participation in national safety campaigns
  • Free public safety demonstrations
📞

Witnessing an Emergency?
